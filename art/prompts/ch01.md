# Prompt pack — Chapter 1: Stint (scenes 1–3)

Style: see `art/prompts/STYLE.md`. Black-and-white ink and charcoal, environment first, no faces, no text. Amber only for real light sources.

## road_beech_wood_noon
Scene c1_s01 — stripping the armour in the wood.
```
Scene: a beech wood at noon, grey light. A covered cart pulled in under the trees off a churned mud road. A heap of plate armour on the leaves beside it, one breastplate catching the light. A small black pond below the trees with leaves on it. At the wood's edge, an empty gibbet with a cut rope. Figures only as distant shapes, no faces. Strictly black, white and grey, no amber.
```

## stint_bridge_dusk
Scene c1_s02 — the bells and the bridge.
```
Scene: a stone bridge into a small walled town at dusk, packed on both sides with a crowd seen from behind and in silhouette, children on the parapet. A covered cart with an empty-saddled grey horse on a lead rein. Six soldiers waiting at the near end. On the far bank, one lit lantern in a window: the only amber in the picture. No faces, no text, no banners with writing.
```

## stint_tollhouse
Scene c1_s03 — the toll book.
```
Scene: a narrow tollhouse built into the middle of the bridge, a lit doorway and a window with a ledger on a sloped desk inside. Four horses tied at the rail outside with grey saddle cloths. The covered cart stopped beside it. No faces, no text on the ledger or anywhere. Black, white and grey only.
```
