# Prompt pack — Chapter 1: Stint (scenes 1–5)

Style (chosen 22 Nov): field sketch, charcoal and ink, from Wren's eye. See `art/prompts/STYLE.md`. Every prompt is one moment as Wren saw it: hands, rope, gloves (Aubric's fleece-lined riding gloves, too big for Wren), the case, the knife.

## road_beech_wood_noon
Chapter 1, scene 1 — stripping the armour in the wood. No amber.
```
A beech wood at noon, grey light, from Wren crouched among dead leaves: in the foreground, a heap of plate armour on the leaves, a breastplate and a pair of gauntlets, gloved hands working a stiff buckle. Behind, a covered cart pulled in off a churned mud road. A small black pond with leaves on it. At the wood's edge, small and far, an empty gibbet with a cut rope. Figures only as distant shapes, no faces. Strictly black, white and grey, NO amber.
```

## stint_bridge_dusk
Chapter 1, scene 2 — the bells and the bridge. One lit window.
```
From the cart's seat at dusk, looking along a stone bridge into a small walled town: crowds packed on both sides, seen from behind and in silhouette, children sitting on the parapet; six soldiers waiting at the near end; the grey horse's empty saddle at the edge of frame. A bell tower over the roofs. On the far bank, one lit window: the only amber. No faces, no banners or signs.
```

## stint_tollhouse
Chapter 1, scene 3 — the toll book. No amber.
```
Close, from the cart stopped at a narrow tollhouse built into the middle of a bridge: an open doorway, a sloped desk inside with a big open ledger (no writing visible, the pages blank or turned away), a heavy hand resting on it. Outside, four horses tied at a rail with grey saddle cloths. No faces, no text anywhere. Black, white and grey only.
```

## stint_square_bells
Chapter 1, scene 4 — the square under the bell tower. One lit upper window.
```
A small cobbled town square at dusk under a tall square bell tower, from low beside the cart wheel: eight riders in grey cloaks drawn up in a line, seen from behind and in silhouette; a horse trough in the middle ground; an inn with one lit upper window, the only amber. No faces, no inn sign lettering, no text.
```

## stint_chapel_door
Chapter 1, scene 5 — the friar and the night's lodging. A single candle.
```
Nightfall at the low door of a poor stone chapel with a patched roof; a single candle burning in a niche above the door is the only amber. From beside the covered cart: an old friar in a patched habit kneeling by a bare foot that sticks out from under the cart's canvas, both seen from behind; two soldiers' shapes at the edge of frame. No faces, no text.
```
