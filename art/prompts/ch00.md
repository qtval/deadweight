# Prologue — The Greywater — panel prompts

Amber arc: a lantern far across (alive) → a smear of it in the broken water → **none** on the bank with the body → Marrow's lantern held high → a thin line of dawn → none → low sun on the road south.

Style (chosen 22 Nov): field sketch, charcoal and ink, from Wren's eye. See `art/prompts/STYLE.md`. Every prompt is one moment as Wren saw it: hands, rope, gloves (Aubric's fleece-lined riding gloves, too big for Wren), the case, the knife.

## greywater_ford_night
Scene s01 — he rides into the ford, drunk. The amber is alive: one lantern far across.
```
From low in the dead reeds on the near bank, at a frightened servant's eye height. A knight on a grey horse, seen from behind, small, slumped, one arm up with a wineskin, the horse just stepping into black, shallow, fast water. Far across, one tiny lantern on the far bank and its broken reflection: the only colour. Ragged night cloud, no moon. A coiled rope lies in the reeds in the near corner. Lots of dark empty space above.
```

## greywater_water_churn
Scene s02 — he goes under. Only a smear of the far lantern in the broken water.
```
From the bank, looking down. In the foreground, Wren's own hands in fleece-lined riding gloves too big for them, clutching a wet rope. Beyond, close, the river boils white and black around a fallen horse; out of the churn a bare white hand reaches up, fingers open, catching nothing. No face, no body visible, only the hand and the foam. A faint smear of distant amber lantern light in the broken water is the only colour.
```

## greywater_bank_body
Scene s03 — the body on the stones. No amber at all: the light is gone.
```
Kneeling on wet round stones at night, looking down along a drowned knight in full plate lying on his back, water still running out of him; his head is turned away, wet hair over it, no face. In the near foreground, Wren's gloved hands at his belt, on a small flat oilskin case and a purse. The river behind, dark, grey moonlight on it. Strictly black, white and grey, NO amber anywhere.
```

## greywater_marrow_wading
Scene s04 — Marrow comes back across the river with her lantern.
```
From low on the bank, past the edge of the body's steel shoulder in the near corner: a lean woman in a hooded coat wades towards us through waist-deep black water, holding a lantern high. Her face is hidden in the hood's shadow. The lantern is the only colour: warm amber, a long broken reflection, a faint amber rim on her hood. A short knife in her other hand, low. Dark treeline, ragged cloud.
```

## greywater_dawn_cart
Scene s05 — grey dawn, the first lie of the day. A thin amber line on the horizon.
```
A foggy grey dawn on the riverbank, seen from where Wren sat all night. In the foreground, a long dark shape on the stones under a cloak, the jaw of the head end tied with a neckcloth (no face). Middle ground: a covered cart with a canvas hood, a woman wrapped in a blanket sitting on its step, seen from the side, face hidden; a lean figure standing apart. Across the river, a mill with no roof. A thin line of amber dawn on the horizon is the only colour.
```

## greywater_breastplate_silver
Scene s05b — the price on the squire. No amber (silver is not light).
```
Looking straight down, as if our head is held by the hair: a dented steel breastplate on a body lying on its back fills the frame, wet gravel around it. Four silver coins being set down on the steel in a little stack by an ink-stained hand; a fifth coin rolling off into the gravel. At the very bottom edge of the frame, close and out of focus, the flat of a knife blade, as if under our own jaw. No faces anywhere. Grey morning, strictly black, white and grey, NO amber.
```

## greywater_wrapping
Scene s06 — they wrap the body in the cart's canvas. No amber.
```
From above and close: gloved hands pulling a rope tight around a long body wrapped in pale stained canvas, the rope biting into it; two other pairs of hands lifting the feet end towards an open cart's tailboard. Mud, trampled reeds, the cart wheel. No faces. Flat grey fog light, strictly black, white and grey, NO amber.
```

## road_to_stint_morning
Scene s07 — the road south. A low amber sun.
```
From the road itself, walking behind: a muddy track winding south over bare hills. Just ahead, the back of a covered cart with a long canvas load showing at the tailboard, and a riderless grey horse on a lead rein behind it with an empty saddle. A huge sky; a low morning sun breaking through cloud on the left, washing the hills in thin amber light, the only colour. Faint smoke from a small town far on the horizon.
```
