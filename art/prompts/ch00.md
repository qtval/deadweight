# Prologue — The Greywater — panel prompts

Amber arc for this chapter: a single lantern on the far bank (alive) → a smear of it in the churning water → **no amber at all** on the bank with the body → Marrow's lantern held high → a thin amber line of dawn → grey, none → low amber sun on the road south.

## greywater_ford_night
Scene s01 — Aubric rides into the ford at night.
```
Scene: a wide, fast, shallow river ford at night, seen from the near bank. Black water over grey stones, running left to right, with moonlight breaking on it in pale streaks. Low wooded banks; dead reeds in the foreground. A thin moon behind ragged cloud.
In the middle distance, a lone armoured knight on a grey horse is just entering the water, small in the frame, seen from behind and side.
On the far bank, tiny and distant, one lantern glows warm amber, the only colour in the image, with a faint amber reflection on the water.
On the near bank at the edge of frame, the dark shape of a covered cart.
Mood: quiet, cold, a mistake about to happen.
```

## greywater_water_churn
Scene s02 — the horse falls; he goes under.
```
Scene: close on black river water at night, violently churned white around a fallen horse. The horse's legs thrash at the top of the frame. Below, an armoured forearm and a bare, ungloved hand break the surface, reaching, the rest of the man under the water.
Chaotic dry-brush strokes for spray and foam, deep blacks around. A faint smear of distant amber lantern light reflected in the broken water is the only colour.
No face visible. Mood: sudden, ugly, absurd.
```

## greywater_bank_body
Scene s03 — the body on the stones. No amber: the light is gone.
```
Scene: a stony riverbank at night after the rain. In the foreground, wet round stones. Lying on his back across the middle of the frame, a drowned knight in full plate armour, streaming water, helmet off but his face turned away or in shadow, not visible.
A small cloaked figure kneels beside him, seen from behind. On the knight's belt, a small flat oilskin document case catches the moonlight.
The river behind, dark, with cold moonlight on it. Strictly black, white and grey — NO amber in this image at all.
Mood: still, cold, the moment after.
```

## greywater_marrow_wading
Scene s04 — Marrow comes back across the river with her lantern.
```
Scene: a river at night seen from the bank. A lean figure in a hooded coat wades towards us through waist-deep black water, holding a lantern high above her head. The lantern is the only colour: warm amber, with a long broken amber reflection on the water and a soft amber rim on her hood and shoulders. Her face is in shadow under the hood, not visible.
Dark treeline behind her, ragged clouds above. Ink and charcoal, heavy grain.
Mood: wary, practical, coming to count the damage.
```

## greywater_dawn_cart
Scene s05 — dawn; the chronicler wakes.
```
Scene: a grey foggy dawn on a riverbank. A covered wooden cart with a canvas hood stands in the middle ground; a horse waits beside it. A woman wrapped in a blanket sits on the cart's step, small, seen from the side, face hidden by the blanket. A lean figure stands a little apart.
On the ground in the foreground, a long dark armoured shape lies under a cloak.
Behind, through the fog, a single thin line of amber dawn light on the horizon is the only colour. Everything else soft greys and blacks.
Mood: hushed, tired, the first lie of the day.
```

## greywater_wrapping
Scene s06 — they wrap the body in the cart's canvas. No amber.
```
Scene: grey morning on a muddy riverbank. Three small figures, seen from above and behind, bend over a long body wrapped tightly in pale canvas and tied with rope, lifting it towards an open cart. One holds the feet. No faces visible.
Flat grey fog light, mud, trampled reeds, the river behind. Strictly black, white and grey — NO amber.
Mood: practical, heavy, unceremonious.
```

## road_to_stint_morning
Scene s07 — the road south to Stint.
```
Scene: a wide view from a hilltop of a muddy road winding south through rolling bare hills into the distance. Far down the road, very small: a covered cart, a walking figure ahead of it, and a riderless grey horse on a lead behind it with an empty saddle.
A low morning sun breaks through cloud on the left, washing the hills in thin amber light — the only colour. Far on the horizon, faint smoke from a small town.
Ink and charcoal, heavy grain, huge sky.
Mood: a long way still to go.
```
