# Base style string — DRAFT (locked at M3)

Paste this verbatim at the top of every panel prompt. Do not edit after M3 without regenerating the 12-image comparison sheet.

```
Black-and-white ink and charcoal illustration, heavy paper grain, dry brush and cross-hatching,
deep blacks and paper whites, very few mid-greys. Grim medieval low-fantasy, rain-soaked,
muddy, cold. Environment is the subject. Any figures are small, distant, silhouetted or seen
from behind; no visible faces. Portrait composition, 9:16, important content kept within the
central 3:4 area. No text, no lettering, no signs, no symbols, no watermark, no UI.
```

Then: the location block (from `locations/<id>.md`, verbatim), then the beat block (what is happening, light, weather, where the body is), then:

```
Do not include: faces, text, color, clean vector lines, glowing armor, dragons, modern objects.
```

## Example (prologue, establishing)

```
<base style string>

LOCATION — The Greywater ford: a wide, fast, shallow river between low wooded banks, grey
stones showing through black water, a rutted cart-track entering on the near side and
climbing out on the far side. Dead reeds. A broken marker-post with no writing on it.

BEAT — Night, rain just stopped, moon behind thin cloud. In the middle distance a riderless
horse stands in the shallows. Near the far bank, half in the water, a dark armored shape lies
face down. A small figure in a cloak wades toward it from the near bank, seen from behind.

Do not include: faces, text, color, clean vector lines, glowing armor, dragons, modern objects.
```
