Black, grey and the paper's own tone; amber ONLY where the scene says. No faces (people from behind, in shadow, or cut by the frame). No text, letters or signs. Tall portrait 2:3, the important things in the top two thirds. Make one image per reply; when I write "next", make the next one.

FIXES TO THE GAME'S PANELS

On the near bank in the foreground, Wren's gloved hands hold a coiled rope, not yet thrown. A faint smear of a distant lantern on the water is the only amber. It should look stupidly shallow.

Wren's gloved hands rest on the case. No amber.
