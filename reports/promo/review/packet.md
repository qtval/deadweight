## Sound (as designed)

- 0–24 s: the river at night, rising; a low string drone comes in under the second shot.
- 24 s: a deep hit and a splash as the horse goes down; the river roars.
- 30–35 s: muffled, as if under water, while the hand rises and closes; then silence for a beat.
- 36–50 s: the river quieter; the drone returns; a soft hit on the body.
- 50–62 s: a heartbeat under the knife.
- 62–70 s: one far bell at dawn; a second, higher string voice.
- 70–76 s: four coins clink on steel; the fourth rolls away.
- 76–84 s: the bells of the town; the strings swell.
- 84–90 s: a deep hit and a low bell under the title; fade out.
