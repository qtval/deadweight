**Not passed.** The automatic checks pass (44/44: length, loudness, true peak, file sizes, caption reading speed, every quoted line and choice word for word in the game).

What the viewers agree on, all three rounds: **the words work and the pictures do not.** The lines they single out as strongest are the game's own ("It took longer to get him onto the stones than it had taken him to die", "Guide hangs.", "He has a fever." / "He'll stink by day three", the choice "Say nothing. Tomorrow he pays you.", the fourth coin rolling away). What fails is the drawing: procedural shapes read as flat paper cut-outs, not ink and charcoal, and the key props do not read at a glance (the horse, the wineskin, the hand, the letter case, the knife on the wrist, the breastplate).

What changed between rounds:

- After R1: opens on a picture, not black; the drowning shows horse and rider tipping under; the hand is smaller, textured, in the river with the far lantern behind; speakers named in the captions; the game's own choice buttons on screen twice (the ford and the knife); Wren's death price made plain ("Then they worked out what I would cost them dead"); horse grey, armour polished; no lantern at the body before Marrow; oilskin case; a charcoal finishing pass on every picture.
- After R2: horse neck shortened, dapples removed; a wineskin; plate edges lit; the case shown on his armoured hip; the knife scene restaged with Wren large in the foreground; a full breastplate for the coins; "He meant to cross the river tonight, drunk" before the first choice; "Nine days to the capital" moved to the end; amber taken off the title text.
- R3 still found: the hand reads as clip art and the caption says it closed while the frame shows it open; the coin shot is unreadable; the knife never touches the wrist; the chronicler arrives unintroduced; ambush lie then fever lie read as two different lies; the dusk town reads as arriving at the capital.

## Decision

The trailer stays out of the title screen until it passes. It is in the build and opens with `?trailer` in the address (https://qtval.github.io/deadweight/?trailer), so the owner can watch it. Three rounds of redrawing by code moved the picture score from 2.33 to 3.00; the remaining gap is the drawing itself, which is what the owner's ChatGPT panels are for.

## Next

Until then, the cheapest gains: the three close-ups the viewers name (hand, case, breastplate), Marrow's knife at Wren's jaw in the coin shot, and one line naming the chronicler before she speaks.
