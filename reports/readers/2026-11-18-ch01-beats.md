# Readers — chapter 1 beat sheet (Stint) — 2026-11-18

The beat sheet in `story/BEATS.md` was read by three blind readers over two rounds. Each also read one prologue transcript, so that contradictions with the prologue would show. Readers: a story editor for branching games; a First Law reader; then, on the second draft, one reader who was both.

## Round 1 (first draft, 16 scenes)

Both readers could say what everyone in Stint wants and what the clock is. Both said the same four things were wrong:

- **It contradicted the prologue.** Aubric's own escort is in Stint and was missing. He arrives in full plate tied in the cart's cover, which is a corpse laid out, not a patient. Nobody asked about the ambush or for the surrender.
- **The clock was spent twice.** The surgeon came at night and again at first light, and one night option (plague) broke the morning.
- **Nobody was hurt.** "The prices are a tariff": a turnip, sixpence, a note in a book. Every bill was deferred to a later chapter. First Law feel if written well: 3 of 5, "about 4 on texture and 2 on danger".
- **Sixteen scenes was twelve scenes of material.** Three "meanwhile" scenes in a row with the clock idle.

Strongest ideas, both readers: the body being better cared for by somebody else than Wren managed ("the smell is better; that is the frightening part"); the toll book with a rate for the dead; the supper.

## Round 2 (second draft, 12 scenes)

Scores from one reader, 1–5: someone can hurt Wren in every scene 4 · decent choices cost on the page 4 · two tempting selfish choices 4 · something goes irreversibly wrong 3 · the new people are people 4 · First Law feel if written well **4**. Nothing lifted. The reader listed about forty remaining contradictions, most of them small wiring (who is where; a variable set and never read) and three structural.

## What the third draft changed

- A new first scene: the armour comes off in a wood before the town. Wren does it.
- Corporal Dobb and the escort (renamed twice: "Brack" and "Tench" both echoed names in Abercrombie).
- The public story is one sentence, a fever taken on the road. The bridge alibi is dropped, because his escort left him at the ford.
- One inspection. The plague option is cut. Refusing or paying the surgeon costs Wren a bowl of blood; fetching the friar spares the arm and makes the debt.
- The night's lodging decides who touches the body: the friar, the old corporal, or nobody but Wren.
- At first light the harms add up: Dobb's mouth always, the groom if Wren added an arrow, the boy if Wren fed him. Taking the Marshal's escort is the one way to free the groom, and the surrender rides on the sergeant's saddle.
- The forged letter moved to chapter 2. Eleven scenes.

**The third draft has not been read by anyone.** It was written from the second reader's list. The first three scenes have been written and read (`2026-11-18-ch01-opening.md`).

## Still open on the sheet
