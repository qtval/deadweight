## What changed

- **The plan is no longer handed over by Isolde.** At dawn she is rattled and wrong first (go back and have it signed again; bury him and say he follows), with Marrow knocking each one down.
- **A new choice under the knife.** Isolde: "Three of us know. I need the guide for the road." She prices Wren's death with Marrow; Marrow has the knife under Wren's jaw while Isolde counts silver onto the breastplate. The player sells the forging hand (and has to write his name on the breastplate, shaking, until it is right), or says his men know Wren's face (and has to say what they call him), or begs (and is saved only because Isolde is three coins short and Marrow will not take a plated ring). 108,864 paths.
- If Wren does not sell the hand, Wren keeps it: "It was the one thing I had left to sell."
- The stakes paragraph on the bank is two sentences; the deadline is said by Isolde in plain words at dawn.

## What the readers still say

- **First Law reader (all three):** everyone is witty in one dry register; nearly every paragraph ends on a button; the knife scene is too short to leave a mark and Wren is arch again two lines later; Wren knows themself too well; the war is names.
- **New player (all three):** the deadline talk at dawn is still the least clear passage, and the dawn scene is where they would put the phone down. Sayle is only a name.
- **Editor (all three):** cut a third of the closing one-liners; the stacked remember-lines read as bookkeeping; stitched "she said" runs.
