## What the readers said

- **Isolde and Coste are the closest pair** (all three). Both are formal, polite and menacing, and both care what is written down. Readers split them by grammar only: Isolde speaks to the squire and sugars it; Coste speaks about the squire to somebody else. A formal line with neither mark was a guess.
- **Wren aloud is the thinnest voice** (all three): "brevity plus a count". "Both skins. I counted." was the only line with a fingerprint.
- **Marrow is distinct in this cast but generic against the genre** (B, C): the laconic cynic with a closing aphorism. The pricing habit is hers; the sayings about life are not.
- **Tobin is a set of tics** (C): run-on, bread, a closing question about the body, five times out of five. "I do not know what T wants."
- **Isolde and Hesper sound like people** (C), because the manner comes from a purpose, not a habit.
- **Uniformity reads machine-made** (C): every line on-model, nobody ever dull or off-key.
- **Frightening:** Coste, for reader A ("the second chair"). **Wanted at a fire:** Hesper, then Tobin.
- Two readers took Marrow for a man. The sheet lines carry no pronoun; in the game Wren's narration says "she".

## Originality (reader B)

Nothing lifted: no name, phrase or mantra. Type echoes to watch:

- **Marrow**: the house style of the books' cynical soldiers (the reader named Tunny, and the Northmen's fatalist one-liners). Mitigation: pricing, not aphorism; she is a woman who sold her own side for back pay, which is nobody in those books.
- **Coste**: "the Inquisition's manner in a marshal's coat" (Sult), and the punctilious marshal as a type (Kroy). The third-person dictation was judged his own. Mitigation: the "Lord-" is dropped from his title; he is petty and left-handed, not silky; his want is a verse in a song. Watch this one hardest in chapter 3.
- **Isolde**: a hired biographer exists in *Red Country* (Sworbreck), "in function only; the voice is nothing like him".
- Hesper, Tobin, Wren: nothing in voice.
- "The premise is more Abercrombie than any single voice is." Already on the watch list in the prologue reports.

## The prologue change: clarity check (one fresh reader, honest route)

- **Who profits if the surrender fails, and how:** answered correctly and fully. "The clearest piece of plot in the chapter."
